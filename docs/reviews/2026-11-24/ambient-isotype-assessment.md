# Ambient-isotype arm assessment

Keep `protein_noise_model="ambient_isotype"` experimental for native protein quantification. Its count likelihood is coherent. Its default fit attributed 58.6% of the fitted signal on absent markers to native protein. Ambient-only had lower native error in all 15 matched fits. These findings apply to the simulated design below. [Benchmark figure](../../_static/adr0012/ambient_block.png) · [Fit-level scores](../../../../mrtotalvi_bench/latent_ambient_20261124/scores/all_scores.tsv)

This review covers MrTotalVI at `7ed740246569c203ef8589f31ee7da74c2e4b870` with scvi-tools 1.5.2. The benchmark used `56eb248`. Only benchmark documentation and figures changed between these revisions. [Benchmark decision record](../../adr/0012-latent-and-ambient-simulation-benchmark.md)

## What the model estimates

For one cell and protein, let $a$ be its empty-droplet profile share. The profile can be pooled or batch-specific. The positive efficiency $e$ varies by batch and protein. The cell has an ambient burden $c$ and, by default, one nonspecific factor $h$. The model sets

$$
A=ca,\qquad N=\operatorname{softplus}(\eta_b+\lambda h),\qquad g=1-\pi .
$$

Here $g$ is the decoder’s foreground weight. The native increment $S$ is $\exp(\alpha)f$ under `scale` and $\exp(\alpha)(f-1)$ under `excess`. The decoder enforces $f\geq1$ and sets $S=0$ on every named isotype. Conditional on the fitted latents, both negative binomials share an inverse dispersion. Their means are $m_0=e(A+N)$ and $m_1=e(A+N+S)$. [Rate construction](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1409) · [totalVI mixture model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7954949/)

The fitted mean is $(1-g)m_0+g m_1=eA+eN+egS$. The decomposition averages those three terms over posterior draws. Its `observed` field predicts the total count. Its `native` field estimates the expected native contribution. The model provides no allocation of individual observed molecules. At fixed latents, the foreground probability given count $y$ would be $g p_1(y)/[(1-g)p_0(y)+g p_1(y)]$. Here $p_0$ and $p_1$ are the two negative-binomial mass functions. The getter reports the averaged gate $g$. [Component means](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1464) · [Public output contract](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1275)

## What identifies the components

**One control constrains a sum.** At fixed efficiency, a control measures $ca+N(h)$. Even with known loadings, one control mean gives one equation for two cell-specific quantities. With two controls, local separation requires two independent responses. The relevant columns across controls are $a_j$ and $\lambda_j\operatorname{sigmoid}(\eta_{bj}+\lambda_j h)$. Nearly proportional columns give little separation. Small counts weaken the information further. Other proteins, shared parameters and priors can constrain the fit. They cannot make a single control an independent measurement of both quantities. Changing the signs of both $h$ and $\lambda$ also preserves the fit and the symmetric prior. [Nuisance parameters](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1262) · [Isotype likelihood](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1425)

**Target abundance enters the estimate of nonspecific binding.** The encoder for $h$ reads the isotype counts and the total across *all* ADTs. Changing only target counts can therefore change $h$. All measured targets also contribute gradients through their nonspecific means. The encoder receives neither a panel mask nor a batch label. A panel missing every control can still produce an $h$ estimate from the total and network bias. The dsb experiments support shared technical variation between controls and background proteins. Whether this particular encoder separates that variation from target biology requires independent evidence. [Encoder inputs](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_components.py#L1250) · [Registration requirement](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1264) · [Mulè, Martins and Tsang](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9018908/)

**Priors and efficiency affect the split.** Empty droplets fix a composition. After efficiency, the observed ambient contribution is $e_{bj}c_i a_{bj}$. Its sum across proteins need not equal $c_i$. The default prior gives $\log c$ mean 0 and standard deviation 2, so its median burden is 1. The simulation planted median burdens of 25 and 75. It also shifted $h$ upward in one cell type. These mismatches can affect allocation. The benchmark does not isolate their effects. The fitted objective charges the lognormal efficiency penalty once for each cell sharing that batch parameter. On the log-efficiency scale $t$, that penalty is $t^2/2+t$ plus a constant. Its minimum is at $e=\exp(-1)$. [Nuisance priors](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1310) · [Efficiency penalty](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L831) · [Planted burdens](../../../../mrtotalvi_bench/latent_ambient_20261124/scripts/01_simulate-data.py#L113)

**The native floor is conditional.** At fixed $\alpha$, `scale` requires $S\geq\exp(\alpha)$. A smaller gate or smaller $\alpha$ can drive reported native signal toward zero. `excess` allows the increment itself to vanish at $f=1$. Both forms aim to estimate the same planted native mean. Their false signal on known zeros counts as denoising error. The benchmark’s floor caveat can explain some error. It gives no reason to discount that error when choosing a denoiser. [Native forms](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1427) · [Native-error scorer](../../../../mrtotalvi_bench/latent_ambient_20261124/scripts/04_score-protein.py#L83) · [Benchmark interpretation](../../adr/0012-latent-and-ambient-simulation-benchmark.md#ambient-protein-models)

**Uncertainty stops at the fitted model.** The two nuisance encoders draw independently given their inputs. Their approximation cannot express a posterior trade-off between $c$ and $h$ within one cell. The public output averages 25 draws by default. It gives no component interval and treats the empty-droplet profile and trained weights as fixed. [Posterior draws](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L736) · [Public decomposition](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1267)

The loss uses the Gaussian KL divergences for $\log c$ and $h$. That calculation is consistent with their latent scales. With default annealing, warm-up also scales the protein reconstruction term. The objective includes the efficiency penalty and a pull toward background on target gates. Training loss alone therefore cannot establish better native recovery. [Loss terms](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L831) · [Nuisance KL](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1310) · [Training objective](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1048)

## Engineering findings

1. **An unidentified isotype weight can change reported target expression.** With the default background exclusion, the normalized-expression getter computes $g(A+N+S)$ before efficiency. The raw-count decomposition reports $egS$. With `scale_protein=True`, the getter divides by the sum over *all* proteins before masking isotypes. For example, take one target contributing 10 and one control with background mean 10. Moving the control gate from near zero to near one moves the target's normalized value from near 1 to near 0.5. Both settings have identical control likelihood. The feature-correlation getter also samples control gates and includes control rows and columns. Correlations involving those controls can consequently depend on an unidentified quantity. [Normalization and scaling in scvi-tools 1.5.2](../../../.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/scvi/model/_totalvi.py#L543) · [Final control mask](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1879) · [Correlation path](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1957)

2. **Missing controls and unmeasured targets get different treatment at different stages.** Registration requires a named control somewhere on the protein axis. A panel can lack all controls. The nuisance encoder reads its zero-filled counts and panel-dependent total. Reconstruction masks unmeasured proteins, and decomposition returns missing target entries as NaN. The target mixing penalty still includes unmeasured targets. A finite nuisance estimate on such a panel can therefore lack direct control support. [Control lookup](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1264) · [Encoder](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_components.py#L1250) · [Loss masks](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L835) · [Output mask](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1302)

3. **Some accepted settings have misleading effects.** The isotype loss drops the stock background KL, so seeding that prior cannot anchor $A$ or $N$. Choosing `protein_background_source="z_base"` changes the source of the native amplitude $\exp(\alpha)$. Neither term in $A+N$ uses that head. The second head bypasses the input adapter required for `batch_representation="embedding"`. I exercised this combination on two cells. The expression getter raised `RuntimeError: addmm: mat1 and mat2 shapes cannot be multiplied (2x4 and 9x256)`. [Prior initialization](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1236) · [Discarded KL](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L841) · [Second decoder head](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1366) · [Embedding adapter](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_components.py#L463)

4. **The mixing penalty accepts values that invalidate the objective.** On a target the term is $-r\log\pi$. A negative `background_mixing_reg` lets the loss decrease without bound as $\pi$ approaches zero. The foreground count likelihood remains finite. The constructor casts this weight to float without requiring a finite, nonnegative value. It also casts `n_nuisance` to an integer without requiring a positive whole number. These issues matter for parameter searches and custom configurations. [Configuration](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1232) · [Penalty](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L835)

5. **Selecting foreground proteins can crash the default DataFrame getter.** I requested P00 and IgG1_iso on a registered protein DataFrame. The name registry was a tuple. Upstream indexed it with a list and raised `TypeError: tuple indices must be integers or slices, not list`. The existing subset regression requests NumPy output, which bypasses DataFrame construction. The foreground wrapper also calls through without setting evaluation mode. Dropout and BatchNorm can therefore remain active when the caller has left the module in training mode. [Foreground wrapper](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_model.py#L1885) · [DataFrame construction](../../../.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/scvi/model/_totalvi.py#L709) · [Existing subset test](../../../tests/test_review_isotype-outputs.py#L48)

**Calibration depends on the raw input.** The calibrator checks counts, aligns named features, and requires 50 selected droplets. Its cutoff compares a multinomial log probability with a threshold proportional to feature count. Even appending all-zero columns relaxes that cutoff while preserving the initial droplet scores. The default 0.995 gives no posterior probability that a selected droplet is empty. The first composition estimate pools eligible raw rows, including called cells. Matched cell barcodes are excluded from candidates. A barcode naming mismatch can leave cells among candidate empties. Removing held-out raw rows before calibration avoids their contribution to the initializer. The calibrator warns about absent ADTs. The optional z-score encoder rejects near-zero empty-droplet standard deviations. Fifty droplets alone give little information about a rare antibody. [Selection](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_ambient.py#L131) · [Absent features](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_ambient.py#L184) · [Encoder scale check](../../../src/mrtotalvi/_module.py#L1277)

**Normal use has several protections.** The dedicated decomposition checks training state and draw count, restores evaluation state, preserves backed row order and drops control columns. Count sampling includes protein efficiency. The deterministic counterfactual decoder rejects this arm. Public biological differential expression is disabled. The decomposition collects five dense cell-by-protein outputs in memory. Sparse calibration limits dense conversions to 8,192 rows at a time. The project permits newer scvi-tools versions despite relying on private 1.5.2 internals. ## What would change the assessment

First, check the public outputs against a fixed fitted model. Changing only unused control logits should preserve every reported biological target value. Masking all controls in a panel should expose the loss of support. Both checks address identifiable engineering failures independently of training quality.

For scientific qualification, compare ambient-only with both isotype forms on independent captures. Use raw empty droplets, matched controls and target presence measured independently, such as by flow cytometry. Include known-negative targets and a weak positive marker. Within each nuisance architecture, hold out an isotype from its encoder inputs, including the total, or shuffle controls within batch. This tests the extra information the controls contribute. Score native signal on negatives, weak-marker magnitude and held-out control prediction by donor and capture. Report uncertainty across those independent units. Cell-level reconstruction and training-seed variation answer different questions.
